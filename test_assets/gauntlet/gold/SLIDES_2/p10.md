# Selection of NEPP FY2019 Highlights

- Increasing focus on advanced packaging
- Maintaining broad efforts in radiation hardness assurance
- Executing SmallSat industrial base assessment (major support from AF/SMC)
- Supporting evaluation and comparison of Fides vs. Physics-of-Failure (PoF)-based EEE parts reliability assessment – university grant kicked off in April 2019
- Examining opportunities for more significant integration of NEPP documentation into future community-consensus products
- Continuing delivery of standard assurance products / services
- Continuing support of NEPAG and GWG teleconferences.
- Selection of product deliveries:
  - BoKs released: graphics processing units
  - BoKs & guidelines to be released: optoelectronics, 2.5D/3D ICs, board-level proton testing, and SEE testing of system on a chip (SoC) devices
  - Other documents in the works: GaN, Small Mission RHA, SOA for Schottky diodes (radiation), SiC radiation testing, update to general proton testing guideline to include medical facility and low-energy guidance, LGA packages, and ongoing radiation and reliability test reports
