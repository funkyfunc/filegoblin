For $m=1$,

$$\sin\theta=\frac{m\lambda}{a}=\frac{(1)\left(550\times10^{-6}\ \mathrm{mm}\right)}{2.5\ \mathrm{mm}}=2.2\times10^{-4}$$

The angle is $\theta=\sin^{-1}\left(2.2\times10^{-4}\right)=2.2\times10^{-4}\ \mathrm{rad}$.

From Eq. 36.1.3,

$$\frac{a}{\lambda}=\frac{m}{\sin\theta}=\frac{1}{\sin 45.0^{\circ}}=1.41.$$
