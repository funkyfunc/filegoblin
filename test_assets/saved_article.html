How Goblins Sort Their Hoard

By the Archivist, 3rd Age

Every goblin knows that a hoard is only as good as its index. A pile of unsorted treasure is just a pile; a catalogued pile is wealth.

The three bins

The classic system uses three bins, described in the hoard theory primer:

  • Shiny — anything that reflects torchlight
  • Edible — self-explanatory, checked twice
  • Mysterious — scrolls, runes, and PDFs
fn sort(item: Loot) -> Bin {
    match item.shine() {
        s if s > 0.7 => Bin::Shiny,
        _ => Bin::Mysterious,
    }
}

Field reports confirm the system scales to hoards of over four thousand items.
